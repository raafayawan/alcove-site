Why Alcove

Built for the library you already have.

No re-tagging, no lock-in, no cloud. Alcove reads your files where they sit and turns them into a fast, private server.

📚

Point it at a folder — or your Calibre library

Add plain folders of ebooks and comics, or mount your existing Calibre libraries read-only. Alcove never touches the originals. On a Calibre source the first ingest of 428,523 books completes in about 14 minutes, inside a single container.

Calibre libraries (read-only) Plain folders Many libraries, one catalogue
🏷️

Your custom columns, respected

Calibre custom columns are auto-discovered and shown as browse facets and detail fields — the thing other apps make you beg for.

🗂️

Every format, as a real section

Books, light novels, manga, comics, and magazines each get their own browse surface with facets tuned to that medium — not one generic list. Saved web articles get a section too.

⚡

Fast and light

A 428K-item catalogue ingests, serves, and sits idle under a hard 3 GB memory cap with no OOM-kill — no external database, one all-in-one container.

One server, every medium

Six sections, each built for its format

Alcove splits your library into sections — each with its own codename in the app — so a manga reader and a magazine reader aren't the same compromise.
